Answer the application question you're stuck on — and put the answer in the box for you. Career Caddy Sender writes application answers in your own voice, drawn from your real career history, and inserts them straight into the form.

If you're job-hunting seriously, you know the grind isn't finding the job. It's the twelfth application form asking you to "briefly describe your relevant experience," and you writing it out again, slightly worse than last time, because you can't remember what you said before.

Highlight the question. Open the extension. It reads your career profile and every answer you've saved, drafts a response in your voice, and offers to drop it into the field. Click Insert and it's in the box — no copy, no paste, no tab-switching.

Answered something similar before? It surfaces that answer instantly, no AI call needed. Your good answers become reusable instead of evaporating into a form you'll never see again.

And it still does capture. Click the toolbar button on any job posting — Greenhouse, Lever, LinkedIn, Workday, a company careers page, anywhere — and the listing lands in your Career Caddy library: title, company, full description, ready to score against your background.

Why install it:

• Answers in your voice, not a template. The draft is grounded in your actual resume, your saved answers, and the job you're applying to — it names real employers and real projects instead of describing a generic candidate.
• Insert, don't paste. The answer goes into the field the question labels. It works on the React and Vue forms that normally reject pasted text on submit.
• It knows when it can't help. If the field is a Yes/No dropdown rather than a text box, it says so instead of quietly filling in the wrong thing.
• Your answers accumulate. Save a good one and it comes back the next time a form asks the same thing — free, instant, no AI call.
• One-click capture. Save the listing without breaking your reading flow, with duplicate detection so you never save the same job twice.
• Optional one-click scoring. Tick "Also score against career data" before you Send and the AI fit analysis starts on the same action.
• Notifications close the loop. A system notification when the job lands, another when the score is ready — click either to jump straight to it.

Privacy in one paragraph: the extension only touches a page while its popup is open — it has no permission to run in the background or on pages you aren't actively using. When you open the Applications tab it reads the text you've highlighted, so it can offer to answer that question. When you click Send it reads the page text, so the listing can be saved. When you click Insert it writes your answer into the field you chose. Nothing else on the page is read, stored, or transmitted, and the duplicate check on popup-open uses the URL alone, never page content. Connect once with your username and password — your login is discarded immediately and only a dedicated, revocable API key persists. The key is named "Career Caddy Sender" and you can revoke it any time under Settings → API Keys, or via Disconnect in the popup. No third parties, no telemetry, no cross-site tracking.
