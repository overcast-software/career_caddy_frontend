Answer the application question you're stuck on — and put the answer in the box for you. Career Caddy Sender writes application answers in your own voice, drawn from your real career history, and fills them into the form.

If you're job-hunting seriously, you know the grind isn't finding the job. It's the twelfth application form asking you to "briefly describe your relevant experience," and you writing it out again, slightly worse than last time, because you can't remember what you said before.

Highlight the question and open the extension. If you've answered something like it before, the answer goes straight into the field. If you haven't, one click drafts one from your career profile and your past answers — and you read it before it goes anywhere near your application.

Your good answers stop evaporating into forms you'll never see again. They accumulate, and they come back the next time somebody asks.

And it still does capture. Click the toolbar button on any job posting — Greenhouse, Lever, LinkedIn, Workday, Rippling, a company careers page, anywhere — and the listing lands in your [Career Caddy](https://careercaddy.online) library: title, company, full description, ready to score against your background.

### Why install it

- **Answers in your voice, not a template.** Drafts are grounded in your actual resume, your saved answers, and the job you're applying to — naming real employers and real projects instead of describing a generic candidate.
- **It fills the box.** The answer goes into the field the question labels, and it works on the React and Vue forms that normally reject pasted text on submit.
- **It won't type over you.** If the field already has something in it — your words, or your edit of a previous answer — it stops and asks. Nothing you wrote gets silently replaced.
- **It knows when it can't help.** A Yes/No dropdown or a set of radio buttons isn't a text box, and it says so instead of quietly filling in the wrong thing.
- **Not the answer you wanted?** Ask again and get a different one.
- **One-click capture** with duplicate detection, so you never save the same job twice.
- **Optional one-click scoring.** Tick *Also score against career data* before you Send and the AI fit analysis starts on the same action.
- **Notifications close the loop** — one when the job lands, another when the score is ready.

### Privacy in one paragraph

The extension only touches a page while its popup is open — it has no permission to run in the background, or on pages you aren't actively using. When you open the Applications tab it reads the text you've highlighted, so it can offer to answer that question. When you click Send it reads the page text, so the listing can be saved. When an answer is ready it writes that answer into the field you highlighted the question for — and never over anything already there. Nothing else on the page is read, stored, or transmitted, and the duplicate check on popup-open uses the URL alone, never page content. Connect once with your username and password — your login is discarded immediately and only a dedicated, revocable API key persists. The key is named *Career Caddy Sender* and you can revoke it any time under **Settings → API Keys**, or via **Disconnect** in the popup. No third parties, no telemetry, no cross-site tracking.
